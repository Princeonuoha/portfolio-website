#Python#Docker#Quant● Paper & demo only

ETH Bot — Crypto Trend-Trading Research

Python + Docker + Binance · 9 services

Research and test bots built around one daily trend rule, N55_M20, running on paper and on Binance's demo account. No real money is traded.

  • Opens a long on a 55-day high breakout (short on a 55-day low); exits when price crosses back through the 20-day low/high
  • Trades 19 coin futures, sizing each position by volatility and capping it at 20% of the account
  • Paper tests with and without take-profit run side by side against live demo orders, scheduled daily
  • Watchdog sends Telegram alerts for HALTs and missed or late runs, plus a daily report; an overview dashboard compares every strategy
